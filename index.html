Policies & FAQ Portal

Everything you need to know about shopping, exchanges, and privacy with Jhariya.

Q: What sizes are available at Jhariya?

We design sizes from XXS (32 inches chest) through XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 4XL, and up to 5XL (54 inches chest). Tap the Size Guide button on any product card to view the complete measurement chart.

Q: What materials do you use?

We source only premium sustainable fabrics. Our collection features Cotton, Pure Cotton, Linen Feel Cotton, and Cotton Silk. All fabrics are pre-shrunk and color-locked.

Q: Can I modify or cancel my order?

Orders can be cancelled or modified within 6 hours of placement. Please email jhariya.ethnic@gmail.com or use the WhatsApp support line immediately with your order reference ID.

At Jhariya, we strive to deliver your designer Kurtis safely and as swiftly as possible. Please read our shipping guidelines below:

  • Free Delivery: All orders above ₹2,000 qualify for complimentary premium shipping throughout India. A flat delivery rate of ₹99 is applicable below this value.
  • Delivery Timelines: Orders take 7 to 10 days to reach.
  • Transit Timings: It takes 7 to 10 days to reach your destination.

We want you to love your Jhariya outfit! If it doesn't fit perfectly or isn't exactly as you desired, our exchange process is here to help (Please note: We do not offer returns or refunds; outfits can only be exchanged):

  • 7-Day Window: We offer a 7-day exchange window starting from the date of package delivery.
  • Condition of Goods: The Kurti must be unworn, unwashed, and in its original state with all Jhariya tags and boutique boxes intact.
  • Reverse Pickup: We arrange a complimentary reverse courier pickup from 95% of pin codes in India. If pickup isn't available, we will request you to self-ship and credit ₹100 back to your account.
  • Exchange Only: There is no option for return or refund. You can only exchange the outfit.

Jhariya ("we", "our", "us") values your trust. This Privacy Policy details how we manage your information:

1. Data Collection: We collect details you provide during order checkout, account sign-up, and newsletter registration, which includes name, shipping/billing address, phone number, and email.

2. Usage: Your details are solely used to complete orders, coordinate delivery logistics, secure payments, and occasionally send festive design previews or discount emails. We never lease or sell your personal details to external marketing agencies.

3. Security: Our website uses Industry-standard Secure Socket Layer (SSL) 256-bit encryption. All financial payments are securely processed by authorized PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (Razorpay/PayU).
